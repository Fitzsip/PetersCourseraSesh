Play #1
—

Draw it up

  1. Put your finger on the ball and draw the play. Run your line through teammates to pass to them, then finish it in the goal to shoot.
  2. The ball goes in straight lines: player to player, then to where your line meets the goal.
  3. Every defender closes in once the ball moves. The longer a pass is in the air, the more ground they cover. The chalk rings show how far they can reach.
  4. You get 3 tries a day. Everyone gets the same play.
