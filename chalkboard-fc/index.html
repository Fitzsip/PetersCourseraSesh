Play #1
—

Draw it up

  1. Put your finger on the ball and draw the play. Run the line through a teammate to pass to him, and finish it in the goal to shoot.
  2. Straight lines are passes and shots. Curve or bend the line to dribble: the player carries the ball along it, slower than a pass.
  3. Defenders chase the ball the moment it moves, and keep running from wherever they got to. A receiver needs a touch to control the ball, so a pass to a marked player gets him tackled.
  4. Offside: when a pass is played, the receiver can't be nearer the goal than both the ball and the second-last defender (the dashed line). The line moves with the defence.
  5. You get 3 tries a day. Everyone gets the same play.
