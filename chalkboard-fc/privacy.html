Chalkboard FC privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version

Chalkboard FC doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your results, streaks and settings (level, sound, play button, reminder) are saved only on your device, so the game remembers them. They're never sent anywhere.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules it on your phone. No server is involved, and you can turn it off in the game's help panel or in your phone's settings.

Internet

The game works fully offline. It doesn't send data to us or anyone else.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: contact the developer through the Chalkboard FC listing on Google Play or the App Store.
