    The plan

    You train every day, but not hard every day. Days alternate: a Build day that pushes you a little, then an Easy day of mobility and core work so your back, hips and grip recover.

    Each minute starts with a beep. Do the reps at a steady pace, aiming to finish in 20–35 seconds, then rest for the rest of the minute. If you can't finish inside 45 seconds, drop 1–2 reps for that exercise.

    Your level

    Change this if a level is clearly too easy or too hard.

    Exercise guide

    Quality first. Stop any exercise that gives you sharp or joint pain. If you have a heart, blood pressure or back condition, check with your doctor before starting.
